# ANEW Retreat & Spa — Wedding & Celebration Packages

> Source: `ANEW_Retreat_Spa_Wedding_Packages_Draft.docx` — draft package structure prepared
> from the August 11, 2026 planning conversation. Pricing and inclusions should be
> finalized by ANEW Retreat & Spa before publication.

A private estate experience in Kenmore, Washington. Celebrate in one beautiful place with
flexible indoor and outdoor spaces, overnight accommodations, and the option to build a
wedding weekend around your vision.

## The Celebration — Starting at $8,000

Wedding day venue rental.

- Private use of designated ceremony and reception spaces
- Access to indoor and outdoor celebration areas
- Use of the estate for your wedding-day timeline
- Getting-ready space
- Flexibility to bring in approved outside vendors
- On-site venue contact for property access and logistics

Best for couples who want the ANEW setting while arranging their own accommodations,
catering, and vendor team.

## The Estate Weekend — Starting at $21,000

Wedding celebration + two-night stay.

- Everything included in The Celebration
- Two-night private estate stay
- Extended access for rehearsal, setup, wedding day, and post-wedding time
- A relaxed home-base experience for the couple and overnight guests
- More time to enjoy the property beyond the wedding itself
- Ideal for destination-style and full-weekend celebrations

Best for couples who want the wedding and overnight experience together without choosing
a fully inclusive package.

## The ANEW Signature Weekend — Starting at $45,000

Elevated all-inclusive wedding weekend.

- Two-night private estate stay
- Wedding ceremony and reception venue rental
- Customized food experience with the resident chef
- Tables, chairs, and event rentals based on selected design
- Wedding-day coordination and timeline support
- Setup and cleanup support
- Customized planning around guest count, menu, bar, and event flow

Best for couples who want one streamlined experience with accommodations, celebration
space, food, and event support coordinated together.

## Customize Your Experience

Available enhancements may include:

- Additional overnight accommodations / extra nights
- Resident-chef catering and custom menus
- Bar service
- Wedding planning or expanded coordination
- Tables, chairs, linens, and specialty event rentals
- Setup and cleanup
- Rehearsal dinner, welcome gathering, or farewell brunch
- Shuttle / transportation coordination

## Reserving Your Date

Packages are customized based on event date, guest count, spaces used, accommodations,
food and beverage, and selected services. A signed agreement and retainer are required to
reserve your date.
